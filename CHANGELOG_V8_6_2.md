# THAMAN POS V8.6.2

- Inventory employee now has both **Submit to management** and **Print count** actions.
- Inventory-employee printout is a blind-count copy: it includes only item identifiers and the employee-entered quantities.
- Expected system stock, stock movements, shortages, surpluses, and all variance data remain hidden from the inventory employee.
- Owner/manager retain the full stock-count notice with variance details.
